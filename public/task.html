Story Generation Task

Scenario

Imagine you are helping a 6-year-old child read a bedtime story. You want to generate a story that matches the child's reading ability and interests.

Task Instructions

  1. Choose a story theme, such as animals, friendship, space, or adventure.
  2. Adjust the difficulty level so the story is appropriate for a 6-year-old child.
  3. Adjust the number of words per sentence.
  4. Generate a story using the system.
  5. Review the story and refine it if needed.

Goal

Create a story that is easy to understand, engaging, and appropriate for a 6-year-old child.

Requirements

  • The story should be about friendship.
  • The sentences should be short and simple.
  • The total story should be around 100–150 words.

Completion Criteria

You are done when you are satisfied with the generated story and believe it is appropriate for the target child.
